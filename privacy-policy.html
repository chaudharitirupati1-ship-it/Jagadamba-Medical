Jagadamba Medical

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains what information Jagadamba Medical (Ambedkar Chauk, Murud, Latur) collects through this app, how it is used, and how it is protected. By placing an order through this app, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your delivery address (only if you choose home delivery)
  • The items and quantities you order

We do not collect passwords, payment card details, or any government ID information. No online payment is processed through this app.

How we use your information

Your order details are used only to:

  • Prepare and deliver your order, or have it ready for pickup
  • Contact you about your order if needed (by phone or WhatsApp)
  • Verify prescription requirements for medicines that need one

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to any third party.

Where your information is stored

Order and product information is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), a secure cloud database service. Access to view or manage this data is restricted to the shop owner through a password-protected login.

WhatsApp

When you place an order, a message containing your order details may be sent via WhatsApp to the shop's number, using WhatsApp's own service. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message once it is sent through their app.

Data retention

Order records are kept to maintain a history of purchases for the shop's records. You may contact us at any time to request that your order history be deleted.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for use by adults placing orders on behalf of themselves or their household. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, you can reach us on WhatsApp at +91 70667 75820, or visit us at Ambedkar Chauk, Murud, Latur.
