About Us

Who we are

Jagadamba Medical is a local pharmacy based at Ambedkar Chauk, Murud, Latur, serving the neighbourhood with genuine medicines and everyday health essentials. We've built this app to make it easier for our regular customers to order what they need — medicines, health accessories, and more — straight from their phone, without needing to call or visit in person for a quick pickup.

What we offer

  • Everyday medicines across pain relief, vitamins, skin care, baby care, diabetes care, first aid, and cold & fever
  • Health accessories like BP monitors, thermometers, oximeters, masks, and more
  • Home delivery or store pickup, whichever suits you
  • Prescription medicines, verified before dispatch

Our promise

Every product listed here is genuine and sourced the same way as what you'd find on our shelves in person. If something you need isn't listed, just reach out to us directly — we're happy to help.
